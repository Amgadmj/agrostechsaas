## 1. Executive Summary & Investment Thesis

### 1.1 Market Inflection: The Regulatory Forcing Function
The Brazilian agricultural credit system (*Sistema Nacional de Crédito Rural - SNCR*) disburses over **R$ 400 Billion annually** across more than 5.8 million rural properties registered in the Rural Environmental Registry (*SICAR*). Historically, environmental and agronomic compliance was verified via static, self-declared paperwork and one-time satellite snapshots at loan disbursement.

This compliance paradigm collapsed on **November 28, 2025**, when the National Monetary Council (*CMN*) and the Central Bank of Brazil (*Banco Central do Brasil - BCB*) enacted **Resolução CMN nº 5.267**, mandating strict statutory compliance starting **March 1, 2026**:
1. **Mandatory Continuous Remote Sensing**: Financial institutions must conduct continuous remote sensing inspections across the entire crop lifecycle for all rural credit operations exceeding **300 hectares**.
2. **"Responsabilidade Institucional Indelegável"**: The doctrine of non-delegable institutional responsibility legally binds banks. Relying on third-party certifiers does not shield lenders from direct regulatory fines, civil liability, and the mandatory loss of subsidized funding equalization (*taxa equalizável*).
3. **The "Área Contínua ao Empreendimento" Mandate**: Remote sensing audits must encompass the entire contiguous operational area, explicitly piercing through artificial cadastral boundaries to ensure that credit resources are applied strictly to the financed enterprise.

Simultaneously, the Ministry of Agriculture (*MAPA*) enacted **Instrução Normativa SPA/MAPA nº 2/2025 (ZarcNM)**, establishing four management tiers (NM1 to NM4) and requiring mandatory contour planting (*semeadura em nível*) on all slopes **> 3%** to qualify for subsidized insurance premiums (PSR/Proagro).

### 1.2 The Technological Void in Legacy Agtech
Existing agtech and remote sensing vendors are fundamentally incapable of satisfying this new regulatory mandate:
- **Cadastral Blindness**: Legacy platforms clip satellite imagery strictly to the single cadastral polygon (CAR) listed on the loan contract, committing massive **False Split errors (Type II)**. Dishonest borrowers exploit this by splitting 1,200 ha farms into four 299 ha CARs to evade audits, or pledging pristine 200 ha parcels while applying the credit to adjacent embargoed or public lands.
- **Unoptimized Drone Economics**: Commercial UAV scouting remains trapped in rigid bi-weekly calendar flights (wasting R$ 80-140/ha/year on clear fields) or naive NDVI threshold alarms that trigger false mobilizations on cloud shadows.
- **Brittle Hydrology**: Standard agricultural drainage and terracing software (Climate Corp, Trimble, ArcGIS) relies on deterministic single-run digital elevation models. Photogrammetric vertical noise ($\pm 20\text{--}40\text{ cm}$) causes "flow-flipping" on gentle Cerrado slopes ($1.5\%\text{--}4\%$), leading to severely undersized contour terraces that overtop, blow out, and form catastrophic erosion gullies (*voçorocas*), destroying land collateral value and violating MCR 2-9.

### 1.3 Agrostech’s Defensible Solution
Agrostech has engineered three proprietary, mathematically rigorous technologies that transform regulatory compliance from a bank cost center into a high-margin underwriting moat:
1. **Área Contínua Multi-Feature Synchrony Fusion**: Fuses optical phenology DTW, Sentinel-1/NISAR SAR backscatter and InSAR coherence, circular crop-row axial statistics, and boundary barrier permeability into a calibrated logistic posterior probability $P(\text{Continuous Operation} \mid A, B)$, proving cross-border operational consolidation under CMN 5.267.
2. **Decision-Theoretic Value-of-Information (VoI) UAV Dispatch Engine**: A formal expected utility model balancing outstanding loan exposure ($E_{\text{credit}}$), phenological vulnerability ($V(t)$), numerical weather cloud forecasts ($P_{\text{cloud}}$), and marginal flight costs ($C_{\text{flight}}$), flying drones only when the information pays for itself ($\text{NVoI} > 0$).
3. **Uncertainty-Aware Probabilistic Hydrology & Erosion Mechanics**: Distributed Monte Carlo perturbation over spatially correlated Gaussian Random Field elevation errors, propagating vertical variance through distributed SCS-CN flow routing to generate per-pixel runoff exceedance surfaces and terrace overtopping failure probabilities ($P_{\text{overtop}}$).

1. **Boundary Permeability ($S_{\text{perm}}$)**: Evaluates high-resolution spatial gradient contrast $\|\nabla \text{NDVI}\|$ within a $10\text{ m}$ boundary strip $U(\Gamma_{AB}, w)$ relative to internal field baselines. Physical fences, windbreaks, and roads generate excess contrast $\bar{\Delta} \gg 0 \implies S_{\text{perm}} = \exp(-\bar{\Delta}^2 / 2\sigma^2) \to 0$. Continuous fields plow through the border $\implies S_{\text{perm}} \to 1.0$.
2. **Phenological Time-Series Similarity ($S_{\text{pheno}}, S_{\text{event}}$)**: Pearson correlation $\rho_{\text{pheno}}$ and Sakoe-Chiba constrained Dynamic Time Warping ($D_{\text{DTW}}$) on regularized 5-day Sentinel-2 NDVI time series, plus inflection date synchrony ($\Delta t_{\text{plant}}, \Delta t_{\text{harvest}}$):
   $$S_{\text{event}} = \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta t_{\text{plant}}^2 + \Delta t_{\text{harvest}}^2}{2 \tau_{\text{sync}}^2}\right) \in [0, 1]$$
3. **All-Weather SAR Backscatter & InSAR Coherence ($S_{\text{SAR}}$)**: Radiometrically terrain-corrected $\gamma^0_{VH}$ cross-polarization curves and 12-day repeat-pass complex interferometric phase coherence $\gamma_{\text{InSAR}} = |\langle s_1 s_2^* \rangle| / \sqrt{\langle |s_1|^2 \rangle \langle |s_2|^2 \rangle}$. Tillage and harvesting cause complete decorrelation ($\gamma \to 0$), enabling cloud-penetrating synchrony verification.
4. **Circular Axial Statistics of Crop Row Azimuth ($S_{\text{azimuth}}$)**: Directional image intensity gradients mapped to circular axial distributions ($\theta \pmod \pi$):
   $$\bar{\theta} = \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{atan2}(\bar{S}, \bar{C}), \quad \bar{R} = \sqrt{\bar{C}^2 + \bar{S}^2}$$
   $$S_{\text{azimuth}} = \cos^2(\Delta \theta_{AB}) \cdot \sqrt{\bar{R}_A \bar{R}_B} \in [0, 1]$$
   Tractors utilizing auto-steer RTK GPS maintain identical heading vectors across the boundary ($\Delta \theta = 0 \implies S_{\text{azimuth}} \to 1.0$).
5. **Calibrated Logistic Model & Graph Consolidation**:
   $$P(\text{Continuous Operation} \mid A, B) = \frac{1}{1 + \exp\left(-\left(\mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{x}_{AB} + b\right)\right)}$$
   Weights $\mathbf{w} = [2.2, 1.8, 2.5, 2.0, 1.5]^T$, $b = -4.50$. Parcels exhibiting $P \ge 0.70$ form connected components in an adjacency graph, consolidating total plantable area to enforce the 300 ha threshold.

#### Decision Formulation
Agrostech formulates dispatch as a closed-loop information-economic optimization:
$$\text{NVoI}(t) = \text{GVoI}(t) - C_{\text{flight}} = \left( \rho^*(\mathbf{b}_t) - \mathbb{E}_Z \left[ \rho^*(\mathbf{b}_{t \mid Z}) \right] \right) - C_{\text{flight}}$$
1. **Prior Belief ($\mathbf{b}_t$)**: Multi-state vector over latent crop states $\Theta = \{\text{Healthy}, \text{Early Stress}, \text{Severe Failure}, \text{Fraud/Diversion}\}$. In the absence of clear orbital observations, belief diffuses toward hazard states scaled by elapsed days $\Delta t_{\text{gap}}$ and cloud probability $P_{\text{cloud}}(t)$.
2. **Action-Loss Matrix ($L(a_i, \theta_j)$)**: Actions include *Maintain*, *Remediate*, *Freeze Credit*, and *Judicial Seizure*. Financial losses are scaled by the effective credit exposure $L_{\text{credit}}(t) = E_{\text{credit}} \cdot V(t)$ in Brazilian Reais.
3. **Phenological Vulnerability Window ($V(t)$)**: Logistic-Gaussian growth multiplier peaking during flowering and pod fill ($t_{\text{peak}} = 65\text{ DAP}, V=1.0$) and remaining elevated during the pre-harvest grain diversion window ($V=0.85$).
4. **Marginal Flight Cost ($C_{\text{flight}}$)**: Explicit model of crew mobilization, transit distance from hub ($D_{\text{base}}$), airspace permit fees (SARPAS/DECEA), and variable per-hectare battery/processing wear.
5. **Optimal Decision Trigger**:
   $$\text{Dispatch UAV } \iff \text{NVoI}(t) > 0 \quad \text{AND} \quad V(t) \ge 0.20$$
   This guarantees that every drone flight has positive expected financial return, creating a legally unassailable audit trail for credit risk committees.

### 2.3 Pillar 3: Uncertainty-Aware Probabilistic Hydrology & Soil Erosion Mechanics

#### The Physics of Flow-Flipping on Gentle Slopes
Under Brazilian Forest Code (Lei 12.651/2012) and MAPA ZarcNM, farms with slope $>3\%$ must maintain contour terracing (*curvas de nível*). However, drone photogrammetry yields noisy digital terrain models ($\sigma_z \approx 0.15\text{--}0.45\text{ m}$) because structure-from-motion algorithms reconstruct dense crop canopy tops rather than bare soil.

In gentle Cerrado terrain ($1.5\%\text{--}3.5\%$ slope), an elevation error of $0.25\text{ m}$ across $10\text{ m}$ introduces an artificial $2.5\%$ slope error, flipping computed hydrological flow directions by up to $180^\circ$. Deterministic models (Rational Method $Q=CIA$, single-run SCS-CN) miscalculate flow accumulation, erroneously certifying undersized terraces that overtop and breach during intense convective tropical storms.

1. **Canopy- and Slope-Conditioned Noise ($\sigma_z(x, y)$)**:
   $$\sigma_z(x, y) = \sigma_{\text{base}} \cdot \left[1 + \alpha_{\text{canopy}} \cdot \left(\frac{\text{NDVI}(x, y) - \text{NDVI}_{\text{bare}}}{\text{NDVI}_{\text{max}} - \text{NDVI}_{\text{bare}}}\right)^2\right] \cdot \left[1 + \beta_{\text{slope}} \cdot \tan(\theta_{\text{slope}})\right]$$
